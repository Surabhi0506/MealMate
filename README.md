# MealMate
About the Project:

MealMate is a multi-nutritionist diet-planning platform that acts as a bridge between customers and nutritionists. It connects individuals who want to achieve their weight-loss goals with nutritionists who provide personalised meal-planning services.

Customers can explore and share their dietary preferences and requirements, and receive personalised weekly meal plans. Nutritionists can manage their assigned customers, create meal plans, and maintain recipes and nutritional information through their individual dashboards.

The platform aims to make personalised diet planning more accessible, organised, and convenient.

Problem Statement:

People who want to lose weight often struggle to plan their daily meals while considering their dietary preferences, allergies, likes, dislikes, and calorie requirements. Finding suitable recipes and maintaining consistent eating habits can be challenging.

At the same time, nutritionists need an organised platform to manage multiple customers, maintain their dietary information, and provide personalised meal plans.

MealMate aims to address these challenges by providing a centralised platform connecting customers with nutritionists.

The system also helps customers keep track of their weekly meal plans and follow their dietary goals in an organised manner.

It allows nutritionists to manage customer details, understand their food preferences, and update meal plans whenever required.

Overall, MealMate aims to simplify communication between customers and nutritionists while supporting better meal-planning management.
